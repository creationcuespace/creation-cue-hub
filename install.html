How to Install Wear OS Watch Faces

Complete walkthrough on downloading, installing, and activating your Creation Cue watch faces seamlessly across Samsung Galaxy Watch, Google Pixel Watch, and other Wear OS smartwatches.

Connection Checklist: Before beginning, make sure your smartwatch is turned on, has Bluetooth active, and is paired to your phone via the Galaxy Wearable (Samsung) or Google Pixel Watch application. Both devices must be signed into the same Google Account.
1

Method 1: Install from Phone or Browser (Recommended)

  • ◉
    On the Google Play Store (phone app or desktop web), find your desired watch face and locate the install button.
  • ◉
    Tap the dropdown install arrow right next to the "Install" or "Buy" button (or look for "Install on more devices").
  • ◉
    Choose your smartwatch device from the list instead of your phone, and tap Install.
  • ◉
    Wait 1–3 minutes while the watch face downloads wirelessly to your smartwatch.
  • ◉
    Activation: On your smartphone, open Galaxy Wearable or Pixel Watch app > go to Watch Faces > scroll to Downloaded > tap to activate!
Tap the arrow next to Install to select your smartwatch directly
2

Method 2: Install Directly from Your Smartwatch Screen

  • ◉
    If you ever encounter an issue installing from your phone, install directly on your wrist:
  • ◉
    On your smartwatch, open the Google Play Store app.
  • ◉
    Tap the search icon (magnifying glass) and search for the watch face name or query: cue, cc, or crc.
  • ◉
    Select the watch face from the results and tap Install directly on your watch display.
  • ◉
    Activation: Once installed, long-press your active watch face, swipe all the way to the right to tap Add Watch Face (+), and select your new watch face.
Direct On-Watch Install
No phone companion app needed. Downloads straight via smartwatch Wi-Fi or Bluetooth.
