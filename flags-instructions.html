HOW TO USE

Flag Complication – Wear OS Guide

Flag Complication lets you display any country flag — plus a custom text label — directly in a complication slot on your Wear OS watch face. Over 150 flags included, fully offline.

Step 1 – Add the Complication

First, assign the Flag Complication to a slot on your watch face.

  1. Long-press your current watch face to enter edit mode.
  2. Tap Edit or Customize (gear or pencil icon depending on your watch).
  3. Tap any complication slot you want to use.
  4. Scroll through the provider list and select "Flag Complication".

Available display styles

🏳️
Short Text
Flag icon + your custom text label
🖼️
Small Image
Flag displayed as a small image only
📝
Long Text
Flag icon + longer text label

Step 2 – Configure Your Flag

After adding the complication, tap it on your watch face to open the configuration screen directly on your watch.

  1. Tap the complication on your watch face — the Flag Complication config screen opens on the watch.
  2. Use the Icon Spinner to scroll through and select your desired flag.
  3. Tap the Text field to enter a custom label — for example your country initials like GR, US, or any short note.
  4. Your selection saves automatically and the complication updates instantly on your watch face.

💡 No phone needed. Everything is configured directly on your watch. No companion app required.

Step 3 – Change Your Flag Anytime

Want to switch to a different flag later? It's easy.

  1. Tap the complication on your watch face at any time.
  2. Pick a new flag or update your text label.
  3. Changes apply immediately — no restart needed.
Tip

You can use the text field for anything short — not just country initials. Try a team abbreviation, a custom symbol, or your initials.

Troubleshooting

The complication is not showing my flag

Tap the complication to open the config screen and confirm your flag selection is saved. If the issue persists, try removing and re-adding the complication slot.

I don't see "Flag Complication" in the provider list

Make sure the app is fully installed on your watch. Open the app list on your watch to confirm it appears, then try editing the watch face again.

The text label is not updating

After editing, wait a few seconds for the watch face to refresh. If it still doesn't update, long-press the watch face and re-enter edit mode — this forces a redraw.
