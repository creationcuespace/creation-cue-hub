🔑 Admin Publishing Guide

🤖 Option A: Approve AI-Generated Drafts

The AI generates articles automatically twice a week. They are hidden from visitors until you approve them.

  1. Double-click create-post.bat to open the Blog Admin Dashboard.
  2. AI drafts appear under "Pending AI Drafts" in the right sidebar.
  3. Click Edit to review and correct the article in the visual editor.
  4. Click Publish Live — it deploys automatically in ~30 seconds!
  5. Or click Discard to delete the draft completely.

✍️ Option B: Write Your Own Article

Write and publish your own articles using the visual browser-based editor.

  1. Double-click create-post.bat in your project folder.
  2. The Blog Admin Dashboard opens in your browser automatically.
  3. Fill in the Title, Excerpt, choose a Watch Face, and write using the rich text toolbar.
  4. Click Publish Live — it auto-commits to GitHub and deploys!
  5. To delete any article, click the red Delete button in the sidebar list.
