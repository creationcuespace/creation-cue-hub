**On your Android device, **open the Google Play app Google Play.
1.At the top right, tap your Profile picture.
2.Tap Payments & subscriptions and then Redeem code.
3.Enter the code.
4.Tap Redeem.
6.Review the account that'll receive the balance you redeem.
7.Tap Confirm.

**Redeem when you make a purchase on Google Play**
1.Go through the checkout process until you find the payment method listed.
2.Next to the payment method, select the down arrow Down arrowand then Redeem code.
3.Enter your code.
4.Follow the on-screen instructions.
