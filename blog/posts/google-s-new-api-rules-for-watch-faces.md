<h2>Google's New API Rules for Watch Faces</h2><p>Google is updating its Play Store rules for Wear OS. By August 31, 2026, all new watch faces and apps need to target Android 15 (API level 35). Existing projects must target at least Android 14 (API level 34) to stay visible to new users on the latest watches.</p><p><br></p><p>Additionally, as of early 2026, the declarative Watch Face Format (WFF) is officially mandatory for all Wear OS devices. For creators building designs in Watch Face Studio or coding companion apps in Android Studio, it is time to review those projects. Bumping the target SDKs now ensures all creations remain fully compliant with Google Play policies and stay active on the storefront.</p>
