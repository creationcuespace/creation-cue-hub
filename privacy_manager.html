PRIVACY POLICY

CreationCue Watch Faces App  ·  Last Updated: March 28, 2026

Creation Cue ("we," "our," or "us") built the CreationCue Watch Faces Android app as a free companion tool to help you discover, browse, and install our Wear OS watch faces. This policy explains clearly what data we collect, why, and how we protect it.

1. What This App Does

The CreationCue Watch Faces app lets you:

  • Browse our full catalog of Wear OS watch faces
  • Send watch faces directly to your watch via the Play Store
  • Save your favorite designs
  • Receive notifications about new releases and promotions
  • Contact us for support

2. Data We Collect and Why

We only collect what we genuinely need to make the app work well for you.

Device Information (via Firebase Analytics)

We collect basic technical details such as your device model (e.g., Samsung Galaxy S24), Android version, and general app usage patterns — for example, which screens you visit or which watch faces you tap on.

Why: So we can understand how the app is being used and improve it — fixing screens that are confusing or prioritizing features people use the most. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or any information that directly identifies you.

Push Notification Token (via Firebase Cloud Messaging)

When you allow notifications, Firebase assigns your device a unique token so we can reach you.

Why: To send you relevant updates like new watch face launches, limited-time discounts, or important app news. We do not use this to advertise third-party products. You can turn off notifications at any time in your phone's Settings → Apps → CreationCue Watch Faces → Notifications.

Support Messages

When you tap the Support button and choose to send an email, the app pre-fills a message that includes your device model and Android version.

Why: So we can help you faster without asking follow-up questions. You will always see exactly what is in the email before you send it — nothing is sent automatically or in the background.

Watch Face Catalog (from GitHub)

The app loads our watch face catalog from a JSON file hosted on GitHub.

Why: So the catalog stays up to date without requiring a full app update. No personal information is sent during this request — it is a simple, anonymous data fetch.

Favorites (Stored Locally on Your Device)

When you "heart" a watch face, that preference is saved on your device only.

Why: So your favorites are remembered between sessions. This data never leaves your phone and is permanently deleted when you uninstall the app.

3. Data We Do NOT Collect

To be clear, we never collect:

  • Health or fitness data
  • Location data
  • Payment information
  • Login credentials
  • Any information from your watch (the app only opens the Play Store on your watch's behalf)

4. Third-Party Services

The app uses two Google services that have their own privacy policies:

  • Firebase Analytics — Google Privacy Policy
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging — Google Privacy Policy

We do not sell your data to any third party, ever.

5. Data Retention

  • Firebase Analytics data is retained for a maximum of 14 months (Google's default setting).
  • Firebase notification tokens are active as long as you have the app installed.
  • Favorites and local settings are deleted when you uninstall the app.

6. Your Controls

You are always in charge:

  • Turn off notifications — Settings → Apps → CreationCue Watch Faces → Notifications
  • Opt out of Analytics — Reset your advertising ID in Settings → Google → Ads on your device
  • Delete your data — Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data. Firebase Analytics data can be deleted by contacting us at the email below.

7. Children's Privacy

This app is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

If we make significant changes to this policy, we will update the "Last Updated" date at the top and, where appropriate, notify you through the app.
