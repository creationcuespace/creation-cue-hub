PRIVACY POLICY

Last Updated: April 8, 2026

Creation Cue ("we," "our," or "us") develops Wear OS watch faces and companion Android applications. This policy outlines our commitment to your privacy and explains how we handle data across all our products.

Products Covered by This Policy

  • Wear OS Watch Faces — Watch face apps published on Google Play, running directly on your Wear OS device (e.g. Samsung Galaxy Watch). Targets Android API 35, supports 64-bit (arm64-v8a), and operates in standalone mode without requiring a phone app.
  • Companion Android App — A phone-side app that complements certain watch faces, providing configuration options such as custom complication text, flag selection, and access to the CreationCue watch face catalog.

1. Data Access and Collection

We prioritize your privacy and do not collect personal or sensitive information for storage on our servers. The following describes all data activities performed by our apps:

Wear OS Watch Face

Health and Fitness Data

Our watch faces access real-time sensor data including Heart Rate, Step Count, and Physical Activity through the Wear OS platform.

Usage: This data is accessed solely to display it on the watch face interface for your immediate reference. It is processed entirely on-device and is never transmitted off your device or shared with any third party.

Wear OS Watch Face

Cross-Device Communication

Our watch faces use the standard RemoteActivityHelper API provided by Google to facilitate actions such as opening a link on your paired phone. A standard Wear OS ConfirmationOverlay animation is shown to inform you when such an action is triggered.

Usage: This is the official Google-recommended mechanism for cross-device actions on Wear OS. No personal data is transmitted through this process — it simply signals your paired phone to open a specified URL.

Companion App

User Preferences

The companion app stores your configuration choices locally on your phone, such as your selected flag and custom complication text.

Usage: These preferences are saved exclusively on your device using standard Android local storage. They are never uploaded to any server.

Companion App

Referrer Information

The companion app uses the Google Play Install Referrer API to identify the source of your visit to our Featured Designs carousel.

Usage: This helps us understand which marketing channels are most effective and ensures relevant designs are featured. This data is non-identifying and is not linked to your personal profile.

Companion App

Featured Designs Catalog

The companion app loads a catalog of featured watch faces from a publicly hosted JSON file on GitHub.

Usage: This is a read-only request to display our latest watch face designs inside the app. No personal data is sent as part of this request.

2. Permissions Explained

Wear OS Watch Face Permissions

  • BODY_SENSORS / HEALTH_SERVICES — Required to read heart rate and step data for display on the watch face.
  • ACTIVITY_RECOGNITION — Required to display physical activity metrics.
  • INTERNET — Used only for cross-device communication via RemoteActivityHelper.

Companion App Permissions

  • INTERNET — Required to load the featured watch faces catalog from GitHub.
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE / BLUETOOTH — Used to detect whether your watch is currently connected to your phone and display its connection status.

3. Data Retention

We do not store your personal, health, or referrer data on any permanent storage or cloud infrastructure.

Health Data: Processed ephemerally in the device's temporary memory (RAM) and discarded as soon as the watch face updates or is closed.

User Preferences: Stored locally on your device only. Uninstalling the companion app removes all locally stored preferences.

4. Data Deletion

Since we do not collect or store user data on our servers, there is no data to delete from our systems.

User Control

  • Changing or uninstalling the watch face removes all face-related local data.
  • Uninstalling the companion app removes all locally stored preferences and configurations.
  • Revoking Sensors or Physical Activity permissions in your device's System Settings stops health data access immediately.

5. Security

We follow industry standards for local data handling. All transient data accessed by our apps is handled securely within the Android and Wear OS sandbox environment. Our watch faces target the latest Android API (35) and are built with 64-bit support, ensuring compatibility with the most up-to-date security features of the platform.

6. Children's Privacy

Our apps are not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of our apps after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
