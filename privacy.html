PRIVACY POLICY

Last Updated: April 8, 2026

Creation Cue ("we," "our," or "us") develops Wear OS watch face apps published on Google Play. This policy outlines our commitment to your privacy and explains how we handle data within our apps.

About This App

This is a free standalone Wear OS watch face that runs entirely on your watch. It requires no companion phone app. The watch face displays health and fitness metrics and allows you to personalize complication slots directly on your watch by selecting a country flag and entering custom short text (up to 4 characters). It targets Android API 35, supports 64-bit (arm64-v8a), and operates in fully standalone mode.

1. Data Access and Collection

We prioritize your privacy. We do not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on external servers. All data handled by this app is processed locally on your Wear OS device. The following describes all data activities:

Health and Fitness Data

The watch face accesses real-time sensor data including Heart Rate, Step Count, and Physical Activity through the Wear OS platform.

Usage: This data is accessed solely to display it on the watch face for your immediate reference. It is processed entirely on your watch and is never transmitted off your device or shared with any third party.

Complication Preferences

The watch face stores your chosen country flag and custom complication text (up to 4 characters) locally on your watch.

Usage: These preferences are saved exclusively on your Wear OS device using standard local storage. They exist solely to personalize what is displayed on your watch face and are never uploaded to any server.

Cross-Device Communication

The watch face uses the standard RemoteActivityHelper API provided by Google to open links on your paired phone when you tap certain elements. A standard Wear OS ConfirmationOverlay animation is shown to confirm when such an action is triggered.

Usage: This is the official Google-recommended mechanism for cross-device actions on Wear OS. No personal data is transmitted — it simply signals your paired phone to open a specified URL, such as our Google Play page.

2. Permissions Explained

  • BODY_SENSORS / HEALTH_SERVICES — Required to read heart rate data for display on the watch face.
  • ACTIVITY_RECOGNITION — Required to display step count and physical activity metrics on the watch face.
  • INTERNET — Used only for cross-device communication via RemoteActivityHelper to open links on your paired phone.

3. Data Retention

Health Data: Processed ephemerally in the device's temporary memory (RAM) and discarded as soon as the watch face updates or is closed. It is never written to permanent storage.

Complication Preferences: Your selected flag and custom text are stored locally on your Wear OS device only. Uninstalling the watch face removes all locally stored preferences.

4. Data Deletion

Since we do not collect or store any user data on our servers, there is no data to delete from our systems.

User Control

  • Uninstalling the watch face removes all locally stored preferences from your device.
  • Revoking Body Sensors permission in your watch's System Settings stops heart rate access immediately.
  • Revoking Physical Activity permission in your watch's System Settings stops step and activity data access immediately.

5. Security

All data accessed by this app is handled securely within the Wear OS sandbox environment. The app targets the latest Android API (35) and is built with 64-bit (arm64-v8a) support, ensuring it runs on the most up-to-date and secure version of the platform.

6. Children's Privacy

Our apps are not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of our apps after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
