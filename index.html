Wear OS Installation Guide

Before proceeding, ensure your smartwatch is turned on and connected to your mobile device via Bluetooth/Galaxy Wearable/Wear OS application.

1 Method 1: Install from Phone or Browser

◉ On the Google Play Store (phone app or desktop website), tap/click the dropdown install arrow.

◉ Choose your watch device from the list instead of your phone, and click install.

◉ Wait a couple of minutes until the watch face is downloaded to your watch.

◉ On your mobile device (e.g. Galaxy Watch), open the Galaxy Wearable app (or Pixel Watch app) > go to Watch Faces > scroll down to Downloaded > tap to select and activate the face.

Important: Make sure to select your smartwatch in the Google Play dropdown!

2 Method 2: Install directly from your Watch

If you encounter issues installing from your phone/browser:

◉ On your smartwatch, open the Google Play Store app.

◉ Tap the search icon (magnifying glass) and search for the watch face name (e.g. search for cue, cc, or crc).

◉ Select the watch face and tap the Install button directly on your watch screen.

◉ Once installed, long-press your active watch face, swipe all the way to the right to tap Add Watch Face, and select your new watch face.
