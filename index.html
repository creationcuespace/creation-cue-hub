FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Common Questions

Everything you need to know about installing, customizing, and enjoying your watch faces.

How do I install the watch face on my Wear OS smartwatch?
+
Open the Google Play Store link on your paired phone or directly on your smartwatch. Tap Install on the watch device option. After installation finishes, long-press your current watch screen on the watch, swipe to the right, tap Add watch face (+), and select your new Creation Cue watch face.
Which smartwatches are compatible?
+
All our watch faces are built strictly for Wear OS 3, 4, 5 and above (API 30+). They are fully compatible with Samsung Galaxy Watch 4, 5, 6, 7, Galaxy Watch Ultra, Google Pixel Watch 1, 2, 3, OnePlus Watch 2, Xiaomi Watch 2 Pro, and TicWatch Pro series. They do NOT support older Tizen watches (Gear S3 / Galaxy Watch 3).
Are these watch faces battery friendly?
+
Yes, 100%! All our watch faces adhere to Google's official Watch Face Format (WFF) standard. They do not run heavy background code, utilize native Android system renderers, and feature optimized true-black Always-On Display (AOD) modes that consume under 2-3% battery per hour under normal usage.
How do I customize colors and complications?
+
Long-press your smartwatch screen and tap Customize. From there, swipe between Color palettes, Hand styles, and Complication slots. Tap any slot to assign your preferred data: Step count, Weather, Heart rate, Battery level, Next event, or custom app shortcuts.
