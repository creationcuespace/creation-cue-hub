User Manual

Welcome to your personal Income & Expenses Tracker. Here is a quick guide on how to use all the features.

1. Adding Records

  • Type: Select whether it's an Expense (spending money) or Earning (receiving money).
  • Amount: Enter the exact amount.
  • Category: Tap to choose from a list of defaults, OR type your own custom category! The app will remember custom categories for next time.
  • Date: Tap the little calendar icon to open a visual month calendar, or tap the text to type the date manually.
  • Location: Tap the GPS pin icon to automatically find nearby stores. You can also start typing to see suggestions based on your past entries.

2. Filtering & Charts

  • Filters: Use the top filter buttons to instantly change the dashboard. You can view All Time, just This Month, or set a Custom start and end date.
  • Charts: The colorful donut chart automatically breaks down your spending by category. Tap on a slice to see exact amounts.

3. Backups & Data Safety

All your data is stored completely privately and locally on your phone. To keep it safe:

  • Tap Backup / Share at the bottom of the app.
  • Your phone's share menu will open. You can instantly save the backup file to your Dropbox, Files app, or email it to yourself.
  • If you get a new phone, just tap Import and select that backup file to restore all your data instantly!
  • The app will kindly remind you if you haven't backed up in over a week.

4. Install as a Native App (iPhone)

You can make this site look and feel exactly like a real app from the App Store:

  1. Open the Tracker in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button at the bottom of the screen (the square with an arrow pointing up).
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Close Safari, go to your home screen, and open the new Tracker app icon!
